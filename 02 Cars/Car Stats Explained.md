# Car Stats Explained

**Confirmed (2026-11-20):** the garage shows the **PR + tier badge** big, then **4 bars**, plus the car's **full upgrade level**.

## 1. PR + tier
Big, top-center, e.g. **B 172**, in the tier's color.

## 2. Four bars
| Bar | What it controls in the chassis | Parts that raise it |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Top speed | Engine, Turbo, Transmission |
| Accel | How fast you reach it | Engine, Turbo, Transmission |
| Handling | Grip, turn-in, braking, drift control | Tires, Suspension, Brakes |
| Nitro | Boost size + strength + refill rate | Nitrous |

## 3. Upgrade level: every part, at a glance
A row of **7 part chips** under the bars, one per slot, each showing its level and the level's color:

`ENG Race · TRB Sport · TRN Race · SUS Street · BRK Street · TIR Pro · NOS Sport`

- Level colors (proposed): Stock gray · Street white · Sport green · Race blue · Pro purple · Elite gold
- A small **"Upgrades 14/35"** counter next to them (7 slots × 5 upgrades = 35 max), so you can tell how far the car can still go
- Tap a chip → jumps straight to that slot in the [[Upgrade Shop]]

PR is the summary number. The bars show *what kind* of car it is (grip car vs drag car at the same PR). The chips show *how it got there*.
