# Car Stats Explained

**Proposed:** the garage shows **PR + tier badge** big, then **4 bars**. Mobile-friendly: no spreadsheets. Detailed numbers hide behind an "i" button.

| Bar | What it controls in the chassis | Parts that raise it |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Top speed | Engine, Turbo, Transmission |
| Accel | How fast you reach it | Engine, Turbo, Transmission |
| Handling | Grip, turn-in, braking, drift control | Tires, Suspension, Brakes |
| Nitro | Boost size + strength + refill rate | Nitrous |

PR is the summary number. The bars show *what kind* of car it is (a grip car vs a drag car at the same PR).
