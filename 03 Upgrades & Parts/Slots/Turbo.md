# Forced Induction (Turbo / Supercharger)

**One slot. Pick one type.** The player must *feel* the difference.

## Types (variants are ideas, list not final)
| Family | Variants | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| Turbo | Single turbo, Twin turbo, Dual/compound turbo | Lag at low RPM, then a big hit of power up top; spool + blow-off sounds |
| Supercharger | Roots, Twin-screw, Centrifugal | Instant power from low RPM; supercharger whine; centrifugal builds with RPM |

## How the "feel" is made (for later)
- Power curve shape changes per type (where the power arrives in the RPM range)
- Turbo lag delay + boost gauge on the HUD
- Unique sounds: turbo spool/flutter vs supercharger whine
