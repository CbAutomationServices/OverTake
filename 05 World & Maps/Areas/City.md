# City (PNW / Seattle-inspired)

Built in Studio under `OverTake_Hub > City` (Streets, RoadMarkings, Crosswalks, TrafficLights, Blocks).
Generated by two build-time modules in ServerStorage: **CityGen** (buildings + props) and **CityBlocks** (block layouts). Old city backed up in ServerStorage.

## Districts
| District | Where | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown core | Middle of the north grid | Glass towers (some stepped, helipads), lobbies with doors, tower plazas |
| Sky Needle park | NW corner (2x2 superblock) | Sky Needle (~550 studs, inspired by Seattle's, original name), round fountain, colorful museum, paths, trees. **No cars** |
| Cedar Square | Downtown | Pedestrian plaza: tiles, fountain, terraces, kiosk, bollards. **No cars** |
| Public Market | Downtown west | Pedestrian market lane with stalls, brick market halls, neon sign + clock. **No cars** |
| Mid-rise ring | Around downtown | Brick + concrete buildings with window grids, storefronts, signs, awnings, water towers, fire escapes |
| Back alleys | Many blocks | Drivable 16-stud alleys with dumpsters, puddles, wall lights, fire escapes |
| Along main avenue | South edge of north grid | Strip malls (2), apartments, shops, narrow shops + parklets beside the N-S avenue |
| South district (new) | South of main avenue, down to the off-road area | Houses with driveways, apartments, strip mall, parking garage, pond park |

## Car-accessible special spots
- **Downtown Garage** (5 levels) and **South Garage** (4 levels): drivable ramps (~10° slope), entrance on the south side
- **City gas station** (west side, near the needle): canopy, pumps, mini mart, repair zone part
- **3 strip malls** with parking lots

## Streets
- 2-lane streets (40 studs) with dashed yellow center lines; double yellow on the main avenues
- Crosswalks + traffic lights at downtown intersections
- Street lamps (dark green), trees, trash cans, hydrants along sidewalks

## Numbers
- ~17,000 parts in the whole hub so far (watch for phone performance)
