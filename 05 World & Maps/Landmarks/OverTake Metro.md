# OverTake Metro

An elevated metro running above the city on a concrete viaduct. **Scenery only:** players can't ride it (decided 2026-11-16). Seattle Link / Chicago L vibe. All dimensions are in [[City Settings]].

## Line 2 — Blue (later)
Waterfront → Old Town (-1290, -250) → South End (-1050, 300) → Off-Road Park lot (-860, 640).

## How the city prepares for it
- Every street the metro follows has a **centre median** wide enough for the pillars.
- **Station lots are reserved:** no buildings in the station footprint. Stair/elevator towers land on the sidewalks on both sides.
- Buildings next to the line can be any height. The viaduct runs above the street, not through blocks.
- Curves keep a minimum radius so trains look right.

## Visual parts (Phase D)
- Viaduct deck with twin tracks, side walls and catenary poles
- T-shaped pillars in the median
- Stations: island platform, canopy, signs, benches, stair + elevator towers to street level, lit at night
- Trains: 3-car sets in a silver body with a red stripe, running back and forth on a timer. Scenery only, so they don't collide with players.
