# Heat System

## Confirmed
- Cops are **AI only**.
- Choosing **night** is a commitment: the only safe way back is a **safehouse**.
- **Busted = lose all cash earned that night.**
- Cop difficulty scales with **your car's level (PR/tier)** + **that night's wanted level**.
- Every race you do at night **raises your wanted level** → bigger and better AI cops.
- **5 wanted levels.** Higher level = **reward multiplier** on Rep & Cash.
- You can **escape** by breaking line of sight.
- Night happens on **separate night servers** (you teleport there when you choose night).
