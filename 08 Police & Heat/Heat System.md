# Heat System

## Confirmed
- Cops are **AI only**, and **essential**: night Rep is the only way to unlock parts, so every player has to deal with them (2026-11-19).
- Choosing **night** is a commitment: the only safe way back is a **safehouse**.
- Night happens on **separate night servers** (teleport in from the Garage).
- **5 Heat levels**, plus a hidden **Heat 6**: stay at Heat 5 for **30 minutes** and it kicks in, exponentially harder (2026-11-19).
- Higher Heat = **faster, tougher cop cars + harder AI** (2026-11-19). It also scales with your car's tier (2026-11-12).
- Higher Heat = **bigger reward multiplier** on night Rep & Cash.
- Every night race raises Heat.
- Escape by **breaking line of sight**.
- **Busted = lose unbanked Rep + a fine** (2026-11-19, replaces "lose that night's cash").
- Gas stations: full repair (C/B/A 1 per night, S/SS/Z 2) + nitrous refill, usable mid-chase.

## How a chase works (proposed)
1. A cop spots you (sees you speeding, racing, or crashing into it) → **pursuit**. The minimap turns red.
2. **Bust meter:** if you're nearly stopped with a cop close by, a ring fills. When it's full, you're busted.
3. **Escape:** break line of sight → the "cooldown" ring counts down. A cop sees you again → it resets.
4. Escape success → small Rep bonus (an escape counts toward Heat too).

## Heat 6
- A banner warns you at the 25-minute mark at Heat 5. Then: "HEAT 6: THEY'RE NOT LETTING YOU GO."
- Everything maxed out, plus rams and helicopter by default. Escape takes much longer.
- The reward multiplier jumps, too. This is the "brag" tier: a badge for surviving 10 minutes at Heat 6.
