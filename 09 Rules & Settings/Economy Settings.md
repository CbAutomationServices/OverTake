## Rep levels
**Rep to reach the next level = 500 + 250 × (current level − 1)**. Max level 50.

| Rep level | Total Rep needed | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Street parts |
| 5 | 3,500 | Sport parts |
| 12 | 19,250 | Race parts |
| 22 | 63,000 | Pro parts |
| 35 | 157,250 | Elite parts |
| 50 | 318,500 | Max (title + cosmetic) |

Each Rep level unlocks the part level for **all 7 slots at once** (proposed).
