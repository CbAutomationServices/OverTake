# Heat Settings

## Per Heat level (proposed)
| Heat | Reward × | Cops chasing | Cop car speed (vs a car at your tier's top PR) | AI | Extras | Escape time out of sight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.0× | 1–2 | 95% | Follows, light bumps | — | 8 s |
| 2 | 1.25× | 2–3 | 100% | Boxes you in | — | 10 s |
| 3 | 1.5× | 3–4 | 103% | Cuts corners, PIT moves | Roadblocks | 14 s |
| 4 | 2.0× | 4–6 | 106% | Coordinated boxing, rams | Roadblocks, spike strips, heavy SUVs | 18 s |
| 5 | 2.5× | 6–8 | 110% | Predicts your route | All of the above + helicopter | 24 s |
| **6** (30 min at 5) | **3.5×** | 8–10 | 115% | Max aggression, no letting up | All + ram trucks, helicopter always on | 35 s |

Cop cars are picked from a "cop roster" per tier, so a Z-tier player gets Z-tier interceptors.
