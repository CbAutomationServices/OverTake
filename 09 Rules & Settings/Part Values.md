# Part Values

Price for every slot × level. PR points come from [[PR Settings]].

**Price = level base cost × slot cost factor**

## Level base cost and Rep needed
| Level | Base cost | Rep level needed |
|---|---|---|
| Stock | free (comes on the car) | — |
| Street | $1,500 | 1 (none: day players can buy it) |
| Sport | $6,000 | 5 |
| Race | $18,000 | 12 |
| Pro | $45,000 | 22 |
| Elite | $110,000 | 35 |

| Engine | Turbo | Transmission | Tires | Suspension | Brakes | Nitrous |

| 1.4 | 1.3 | 1.0 | 0.8 | 0.9 | 0.7 | 0.9 |
