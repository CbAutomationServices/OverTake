# Garage

## Confirmed
- The garage is the gateway: travel to other maps, choose day/night, and join races all happen here
- The regular menu can show what events are running on each map, but can't join them

## What's on screen (proposed)
- Car on a turntable, center. **PR + tier badge** top-center, big (e.g. **C 84**)
- 4 stat bars (Speed / Accel / Handling / Nitro) → [[Car Stats Explained]]
- Bottom row of big buttons: **Upgrade** · **Customize** · **Race (Day)** · **🌙 Night** · **Travel** · **Cars**
- The Night button glows once it's unlocked → [[First 10 Minutes]]
