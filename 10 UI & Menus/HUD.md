# HUD

## What's on screen (proposed)
- **Bottom right:** speed + gear + nitro bar
- **Top center:** position, lap, gap to the next car
- **Combo meter:** floats near the car while drifting or near-missing
- **Night only:** Heat level (1–5, 6 in red), plus the **bust ring** and **escape ring** around the minimap
- **Phone:** steering left; gas/brake right; big NITRO and HANDBRAKE buttons
- Hide chat and menus during races
