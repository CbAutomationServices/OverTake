# Nature Prefabs

- **Where:** `ServerStorage.Prefabs.Nature`
- **Source:** free Creator Store meshes. These are mesh only: every script was removed on import.
  - Ponderosa Pine Pack (Letaij)
  - Western Red Cedar (Letaij), with the moss meshes removed for performance
  - Realistic Pine Tree (GabeQZ)
  - Low-poly boulder pack (Glitzcrank5835)
  - Four Bush Types (Gearbyte4131)
- **Models:**
  - 12 firs: `Tree_Fir1-12`
  - `Tree_TallPine`
  - `Tree_Cedar`
  - 23 rocks
  - 4 bushes
- **Collisions:** trees have an invisible trunk collider (cars hit the trunk, not the leaves). Rocks are solid.
- **Placed:** `Workspace.OverTake_Hub.Nature`, about 3,000 trees, 970 rocks and 1,430 bushes. They're scattered by noise into forests and clearings, kept off roads, trails, the creek and built areas, with a tree line around elevation 200.
- **Old part-built trees:** moved to `ServerStorage.OldPartTrees`.
